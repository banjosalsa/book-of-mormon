Book of Mormon geography web

Every direction, distance and place reference in the text, linked into one graph and tested for contradictions. Built only from the text, with no outside maps.

Places are positioned to show links, not real locations.

Click a place to see every relation and verse that involves it. Click a link to see its evidence. Drag nodes to rearrange; scroll to zoom.
